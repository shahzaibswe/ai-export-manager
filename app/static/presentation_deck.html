The Pain Point

The Exporter Language & Know-How Barrier

1. Language Gap

Exporters in Sialkot (leather, surgical, sports) and Faisalabad (textiles) describe and sell goods in Urdu or Roman Urdu. Global buyers expect keyword-dense commercial English.

2. Listing Rules

Alibaba and Amazon enforce strict character counts, 5 benefit-driven bullets, and backend SEO tags. Poor listings get buried in marketplace search algorithms.

3. Pricing Blindspot

Exporters lack visibility into US/EU market willingness-to-pay and lose 20-30% margins. Furthermore, navigating Payoneer vs Wise and FBR 0.25% export tax is confusing.

Our Innovation

End-to-End Urdu-to-Global Export Pipeline

Voice / Urdu Input

Natural voice note or typed Roman Urdu input with number & unit normalization.

Smart Urdu Slot Loop

Asks missing details (MOQ, material, sizes) in conversational Urdu specific to the product.

RAG Generator

Top-seller style retrieval creates titles, 5 benefit bullets, and 15 SEO keywords.

1-Click Publish

Publishes live to simulated Alibaba Trade Showcase and Amazon SP-API catalog.

NLP Core

7-Stage NLP Pipeline & Quality Rubric Loop

0. Capture & Normalization: Roman Urdu clean + verbal numbers ('paanch sau' -> 500)
1. NER Attribute Extraction: Pydantic schema (product, material, sizes, colors, MOQ, price)
2. Smart Slot Check: Missing fields trigger conversational Urdu follow-up question
3. RAG Listing Generator: Few-shot top-seller styles generate title, 5 bullets, 15 keywords
4. Platform Formatter: Alibaba RFQ (128 chars) & Amazon SP-API schema limits
